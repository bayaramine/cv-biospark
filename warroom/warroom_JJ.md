| Question | Key talking points |
|----------|-------------------|
| What was CARTITUDE-2 trying to show? | Phase II multicohort exploration of cilta-cel (BCMA CAR-T) across multiple earlier-line settings: lenalidomide-refractory 1–3L, early relapse, post-anti-BCMA, and NDMM suboptimal ASCT response. Each cohort tested the hypothesis that deep MRD-negative responses seen in heavily pretreated patients (CARTITUDE-1) could be reproduced earlier in the disease course. |
| What is MRD negativity and why is it the primary endpoint? | MRD = measurable residual disease; assessed by NGS at 10⁻⁵ sensitivity (1 myeloma cell per 100,000 normal cells). In myeloma, MRD negativity is the deepest measurable response and strongly predicts long-term PFS/OS. It's more sensitive than sCR by standard IMWG criteria and can detect residual disease even in patients in clinical CR. |
| What were the main findings? | Remarkable efficacy in early lines: Cohort A ORR 95%, MRD neg 100% of evaluable, 24-month PFS 75%. Cohort B ORR 100%, MRD neg 93%. Even in heavily pretreated post-BCMA patients (Cohort C), ORR 60% with median PFS 9 months. Cohort D NDMM: 94% ORR, >90% 3-year PFS/OS. |
| What is the statistical complexity in a single-arm phase II? | No comparator arm so no inferential testing — but precision of estimates matters. Exact binomial CIs for response rates (Clopper-Pearson). KM for PFS/OS with small N means wide CIs and censoring-heavy plots. MRD as primary requires defining evaluability criteria carefully (patients with sufficient bone marrow sample). Multiple cohorts analyzed independently with no multiplicity adjustment — each cohort stands alone. |
| How was the manufacturing change in Cohort A handled? | Cohort A enrolled an initial subgroup with clinical trial manufacturing, then an expansion with commercial manufacturing. Both subgroups analyzed separately due to potential differences in product characteristics. Reporting both transparently was critical for regulatory credibility. |
| What happened with cilta-cel neurotoxicity? | CARTITUDE studies identified a specific CAR-T neurotoxicity — movement and neurocognitive disorder (MNT) — distinct from classical ICANS. Rates were generally low (5–15%) but required careful monitoring and reporting. |

For a combination study like TRIlogy-2 with multiple regimens, BOIN's simplicity and simultaneous multi-track operation makes it practical. |
